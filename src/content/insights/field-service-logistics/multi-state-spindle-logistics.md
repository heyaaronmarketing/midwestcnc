## Key Takeaways

- Shipping a spindle from Texas to Waterloo, Iowa covers 870 to 1,100 miles, and freight runs $280 to $650 for most LTL shipments under 100 pounds.
- Proper packing means a wooden crate or double-wall box with 3 inches of foam on all sides to protect bearing preload.
- Total time from Texas pickup to rebuilt spindle return runs 13 to 22 days, with bench work taking 7 to 12 days of that window.
- Spindle weight sets the freight class: a 45-pound VMC spindle costs less to ship than a 180-pound HBM unit.
- Photos of the taper face and rear housing before packing create a baseline for damage claims and speed up diagnosis.

A Texas shop that needs to ship a CNC spindle for repair faces a 900-mile journey. That distance matters. Spindles are precision parts. Angular contact bearings, preload settings, and taper geometry can all suffer from poor handling. The good news: with the right crate and carrier, spindles arrive at our Waterloo bench ready to rebuild every week. This guide covers the real numbers for getting a spindle from Texas to Iowa and back.

## Miles and Days on the Road

Texas to Waterloo spans real distance. Houston sits about 1,050 miles away. Dallas-Fort Worth is closer at 870 miles. San Antonio runs about 1,100 miles. These routes take 3 to 5 business days with standard LTL freight.

Transit time is time your machine sits idle. We quote bench time and transit time as separate items. A [Mazak Spindle Repair](/spindle-grinding/mazak-spindle-repair/) that needs new bearings and balancing takes 7 to 12 days on the bench. Add 3 to 5 days each way for shipping. The full window runs 13 to 22 days from Texas pickup to returned spindle.

Shops with tight deadlines should plan for this full range. Some Texas plants keep a rebuilt spare on the shelf. They ship the failed unit, install the spare, and swap inventory when the rebuild comes back. That approach cuts downtime to the reinstall window alone.

## What Freight Costs from Texas

Spindle weight and size set the freight class. LTL carriers with lift-gate service handle most jobs. Pricing follows clear patterns.

A compact VMC spindle in the 35 to 50 pound range ships for $280 to $450 from Dallas. Houston runs higher—closer to $400 to $650—due to the extra miles. These weights cover many [Haas ST Series](/spindle-grinding/haas-spindle-repair/st-series/) lathes and smaller Fanuc-controlled machines.

Heavier spindles cost more. A horizontal boring mill spindle at 150 to 200 pounds moves into a higher freight class. These units run $450 to $800 on the same Texas-to-Iowa route. The wooden crate adds weight and footprint.

Very large spindles—300 pounds or more from big HMCs—may need flatbed or special handling. We have seen $900 to $1,400 for Texas origins on these units. Get a quote before you commit.

## How to Pack a Spindle for 900 Miles

Packaging for spindle shipping is stricter than for most machine parts. Bearings are ground to tight specs. Preload is set in microns. Impact can brinell races, shift preload, or nick the taper face.

Cover the taper face with foam before anything else. Use a fitted plastic cap or wrap foam sheeting around it and tape it tight. The taper is where the toolholder meets the spindle. Even small dings mean re-grinding.

Use a wooden crate or double-wall box. Single-wall cardboard fails too often in LTL freight. Packages get stacked and shifted. Wood is the standard for spindles over 75 pounds.

Pack 3 inches of foam on all six sides. We have seen spindles arrive with 1 inch of foam and new bearing damage. The carrier's claim process will not pay for the extra repair work.

Mark the crate clearly. "This End Up" and "Fragile — Precision Machinery" labels help handlers. Ship taper-face-up when you can.

## Take Photos Before You Pack

Spend 5 minutes on photos before you wrap the spindle. This step does two things: it sets a baseline for damage claims, and it gives us useful data before we open the housing.

Shoot the taper face. Look for scoring, rust, or dings. Shoot the rear bearing housing. If the drawbar or encoder mount shows damage, capture that too.

Note the machine make, model, and serial number. Include the spindle part number if you can read it. For [Fanuc Spindle Repair](/spindle-grinding/fanuc-spindle-repair/), knowing if the spindle came from a [Series 0i](/spindle-grinding/fanuc-spindle-repair/series-0i/) or [Series 30i / 31i](/spindle-grinding/fanuc-spindle-repair/series-30i-31i-32i/) helps us stage bearing kits and encoder parts.

Write down the failure symptoms. Note vibration at certain RPM ranges, noise type, runout if you have it, and thermal issues. This speeds diagnosis.

Put the notes in a waterproof bag inside the crate. Email us a copy as well.

## Picking the Right Carrier

Not all carriers handle precision freight well. We track arrival condition across carriers. Some patterns are clear.

Lift-gate service is essential for most shop docks. Many Texas plants lack docks at standard trailer height. Dropping a spindle crate from 48 inches is the kind of impact that ruins bearings.

Inside pickup helps when the spindle is far from the dock. This adds $40 to $75 but saves you from moving a 150-pound crate through the shop.

We can book freight from Texas as part of the repair job. This keeps the spindle on carriers we trust. If you prefer your own freight account, just send us the tracking number.

For [Okuma MB / MA Vertical](/spindle-grinding/okuma-spindle-repair/mb-ma-verticals/) spindles in the 75 to 120 pound range, we suggest carriers that focus on industrial freight. Their handling rules are better than general package carriers.

## Return Shipping and Getting It Back in the Machine

The rebuilt spindle ships back the same way—same carrier type, same packing grade. We crate spindles in-house before return, so quality stays consistent.

Return transit takes the same 3 to 5 days. We time the ship date so the spindle lands when your crew is ready. Not on a Friday before a long weekend.

Reinstall is usually a shop-side task for Texas. Our standard drive radius does not reach that far. The rebuilt spindle comes with runout data, preload specs, and install notes. For shops with millwrights or trained techs, this is enough.

If your team wants phone support during reinstall, we offer that. We can walk through alignment, drawbar torque, and break-in steps while your tech works.

For complex jobs—encoder timing or drive sync on [CNC Spindle Repair, Rebuilds & Grinding](/spindle-grinding/) work—some shops ask us to fly in for the reinstall. We quote these case by case. It makes sense when the machine is critical.
